How to Play

  1. Start with 26 face-down cards each.
  2. Click Flip Cards. The higher card wins both cards and adds them to the bottom of its pile.
  3. Ranks: 2–10, Jack, Queen, King, Ace. Suits do not matter.
  4. A tie starts WAR! Click again to place one card face down and one face up per side. The higher face-up card wins the whole battle.
  5. Another tie repeats war. With only one card, reveal it directly. A side with no cards loses and the opponent takes the battle. If both run out in an unresolved war, it is a draw.
  6. Win all 52 cards to win the game.

Restart Game shuffles and deals a fresh game. War can last a long time.
